What I do

• I build clean, good-looking frontend websites for people who want to showcase their work, including portfolios, personal pages, project showcases, resumes, or simple one page profiles about you.

• These are pure frontend sites, built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript only. No backend, no database, no server side complexity. Just a fast, lightweight site that loads instantly and looks great on any device.

• Perfect for anyone who needs a single page (or a few pages) containing your details, work, links, and information: the kind of site you'd share with a client, recruiter, or on social media.

• Sites are hosted on GitHub Pages, and if you'd like, I can also set up a custom domain so it's your own branded link instead of a github.io URL.

• If you're unsure whether your website idea can be built by me, feel free to DM me on my social media.

About Me

Who I am

• I'm VAS, 18 years old, currently based in India.

I know HTML, CSS, JavaScript, C#, and full stack development, but for now I only offer frontend based websites with no backend, no server, and no database.

I have 5 years of experience in overall programming: 3 years of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and 2 years of .NET Core web development.

I also create assets for the websites I make, such as icons, logos, and a bit of photo editing.
